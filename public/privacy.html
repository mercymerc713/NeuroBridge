NeuroBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Summary

NeuroBridge is designed for neurodivergent learners. We respect your privacy and your child's privacy. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Storage

All user data — profiles, progress, settings, custom lessons, and soundboard usage — is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. No data leaves your device unless you manually export it using the backup feature.

No Accounts or Sign-Ups

NeuroBridge does not require an account, email address, or any personally identifiable information to use.

No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No cookies are used for tracking purposes.

Text-to-Speech

NeuroBridge uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (Web Speech API) to read words and sentences aloud. Speech processing happens entirely on your device. No audio data is sent to external servers.

Google Fonts

NeuroBridge loads fonts from Google Fonts to improve readability. Google may log standard web request data (IP address, browser type) when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Once cached, fonts load offline without contacting Google.

Offline Use

NeuroBridge is designed to work offline after the initial load. A service worker caches the app and font files so the app remains fully functional without an internet connection.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

NeuroBridge is designed for users of all ages, including children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Data Export & Deletion

Parents and caregivers can export all data as a JSON file from the Profiles screen. To delete all data, clear the browser's localStorage for this app or use the browser's "Clear site data" feature. Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of NeuroBridge after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
